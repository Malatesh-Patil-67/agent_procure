# Supplier Allocation Intelligence

This context supports procurement teams deciding how to distribute component demand among suppliers. Recommendations are reviewable evidence, not purchase orders.

## Language

**Supplier commitment**:
A contractual or certified capability promised by a supplier, such as price, capacity, lead time, quality threshold, or certification validity.
_Avoid_: Claim, declaration

**Observed performance**:
A measured operational outcome from delivery, quality, capacity, or incident records.
_Avoid_: Actual behaviour, realised result

**Verification assessment**:
A comparison of supplier commitments with observed performance that produces a deterministic status and findings.
_Avoid_: Supplier score, recommendation

**Evidence reference**:
An auditable document passage or operational-data record supporting a commitment, observed performance, or assessment.
_Avoid_: Source, citation

**Allocation scenario**:
A proposed distribution of quarterly component demand across eligible suppliers under explicit sourcing constraints.
_Avoid_: Purchase order, sourcing decision

**Procurement review**:
A human decision to approve, reject, or request changes to an allocation scenario or verification assessment.
_Avoid_: Agent approval, automatic decision
